Nuova espressione / New expression

"Da + ruolo/condizione" To indicate a specific time, role, or condition both in the past and in the present.

🕒 Nel passato / In the past

  • da bambina / bambino = when I was a child 🧒
  • da giovane = when I was young 🧑
  • da studentessa = when I was a student (in the past) 📚

🎯 Nel presente / In the present

  • da studentessa / studente = as a student (now, from a student's point of view) 📝
  • da turista = as a tourist / traveling as a tourist 🗺️
  • da amico / amica = as a friend *(e.g., "Ti parlo da amico")* 🤝
📚

Lessico nuovo / New vocabulary

  • Dare / Passare ✋ to give / to pass
  • Regalare 🎁 to give a present/gift (per un compleanno o un'occasione)
  • Prestare 🤝 to lend (qualcosa che poi ritorna indietro / something you will get back)
